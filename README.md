### Hey, I'm Serhii 👋

I build onchain apps end to end: smart contracts, the app on top, and shipping it to real users.
Right now I'm most interested in **L2 interoperability** - what happens to messages and funds moving between rollups and Ethereum, and how to detect when they get stuck.

**Projects**

- **[DeadBags](https://deadbags.com)** - finds forgotten funds in dead protocols. Tracks unfinished and unclaimed L2→L1 bridge withdrawals on Blast, Abstract and Sophon, plus friend.tech V1 (Base) and Alpaca (BNB). Reads contracts at the latest block, simulates the exit from your address before showing anything, and gives a proof for every number. Read-only, no wallet connection. *v0.1 live, in active development (closed source for now).*
- **[Lock-In](https://github.com/serhiomaserati/lock-in)** - commitment staking for Solana Seeker. Lock SKR on a habit goal, check in daily, get it back if you keep your word. Anchor program + React Native app, live in the Solana dApp Store. [lockinhq.app](https://lockinhq.app)
- **[HedgeHub](https://github.com/serhiomaserati/one-click-hedge)** - hedge any Polymarket position in one click at live order-book prices, with an AI risk read on your whole book. Next.js, CLOB V2, non-custodial. [hedgehub.app](https://hedgehub.app)
- **[Base Blast](https://github.com/serhiomaserati/base-blast)** - arena shooter as a Base mini app. Deterministic sim replayed on the server so scores can't be faked, EIP-712 signed submits, gasless via paymaster (EIP-5792).
